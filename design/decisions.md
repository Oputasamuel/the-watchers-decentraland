# Design Decisions

- **2026-11-05 · Working direction:** General game design, doc-only planning for the next concept. **Owner call:** THE ASCENT is no longer the active design after rejection. The active aim is a bicycle meal-delivery game in Decentraland: pick up meals and ride them to waiting customers. It must use a normal third-person rider/chase camera; a top-down camera is a standing non-goal. The prior GDD remains archived for history. **Next open decision:** the setting and delivery-loop rules that make the game distinct.
- **2026-11-05 · Mount flow:** **Owner call:** players mount at a physical take-off/mount terminal and can return to a terminal to dismount. The city must serve as the navigable delivery map, not merely a backdrop. **Open:** city identity, district layout, and the first-delivery route.
- **2026-11-05 · City asset base:** **Owner call:** use *City Builder Free Tiles* by Measure Studios as the city’s base asset kit. It provides the visual language for roads, pavements, buildings, and navigation. **Constraint:** use the CC0 source files and retain source/licence provenance; do not download/import until build work is authorised.
- **2026-11-05 · City atmosphere:** **Owner call:** set the delivery game in a warm evening city with streetlights and food markets. Streetlight corridors, storefront glow, and market activity should support navigation and mood; the game remains non-futuristic.
- **2026-11-05 · Street challenge:** **Owner call:** combine route-finding with traffic/hazards. **Design implication:** traffic must be light, legible, and mobile-friendly; learning shortcuts remains as important as avoiding obstacles. Specific hazard rules remain open.
- **2026-11-05 · Delivery outcomes:** **Owner call:** every order has a time limit; expiry cancels the ride. A crash still permits delivery but pays poorly. **Open:** exact timer ranges, crash recovery time, and the score/currency system.
- **2026-11-05 · Order flow:** **Owner call:** the game assigns the next order automatically rather than offering an order-selection menu. **Design implication:** each completed, failed, or cancelled order quickly resolves into the next pickup; difficulty can rise through the shift without blocking the rider in UI.
- **2026-11-05 · Shift length:** **Owner call:** shifts are endless. Players continue receiving automatic orders until they voluntarily return to the Rider Hub. **Open:** what persists or is displayed when a rider ends a shift.
- **2026-11-05 · Social structure:** **Owner call:** every player receives and completes their own deliveries. The social layer is a high-score board that ranks riders by delivery performance, not shared/co-op orders. **Design implication:** the full loop works alone while other riders provide visible comparison and motivation. **Open:** exact ranking metric and reset period.
- **2026-11-05 · Ranking metric:** **Owner call:** rank riders by the count of successfully completed deliveries. Tips, delivery speed, and crash state do not alter leaderboard rank. **Open:** leaderboard reset period and whether lifetime personal-best data is also shown.
- **2026-11-05 · Leaderboard cadence:** **Owner call:** use an all-time leaderboard; it never resets. **Design implication:** the game needs durable player-score storage and may separately display the local rider's own count/progress. A new player must still have satisfying personal milestones despite the established board.
- **2026-11-05 · Earnings scope:** **Owner call:** earnings are feedback only in the first version; they unlock nothing. **Non-goal for first playable:** no bike stat upgrades, cosmetic economy, or personal-garage system.
- **2026-11-05 · Delivery handoff:** **Owner call:** use both a visible delivery marker and a waiting customer NPC. **Design implication:** the marker must remain clear at riding distance; the NPC and a short handoff reaction supply the close-range payoff.
- **2026-11-05 · Handoff input:** **Owner call:** delivery handoff completes automatically when a rider enters the marked customer zone. **Design implication:** no confirmation input or dismount is required, preserving continuous bike flow.
- **2026-11-05 · Crash recovery:** **Owner call:** a crash resets the rider to their last safe point. The order stays active, but any completed delivery after a crash pays poorly. **Open:** safe-point placement and reset timing must be tested for fairness on mobile.
- **2026-11-05 · World structure:** **Owner call:** build one continuous city map. **Non-goal:** separate delivery maps, district-selection screens, or level-loading transitions. Neighborhoods may exist as connected parts of the same city.
- **2026-11-05 · Naming direction:** **Owner preference:** the title should use or evoke “Miles.” **Open:** final public title.
- **2026-11-05 · Public title:** **Owner call:** the game is titled **Miles**.
- **2026-11-05 · Per-order route:** **Owner call:** each automatic order chooses a random city pickup spot and a separate customer destination. The rider travels to the pickup, collects the meal, then delivers it; the Rider Hub is only for mount/dismount and ending a shift, not a mandatory between-order return.
- **2026-11-05 · Interaction UI:** **Owner call:** use custom, context-aware in-game action buttons instead of Decentraland's default E prompt. **Mobile constraint:** buttons must be large touch targets; desktop players can click the same UI. **Open:** exact layout and visual style.
