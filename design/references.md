# Design References

## City kit

- **City Builder Free Tiles — Measure Studios**  
  https://mxfilho.itch.io/city-builder-free-tiles  
  **Owner interest:** selected as the base city kit for the bicycle meal-delivery game. The pack lists 25+ low-poly models, road tiles, pavement, buildings, fences, trees, and GLB/FBX/OBJ formats under CC0. Use it for readable, modular delivery routes; it does not decide the game’s setting or rules.
